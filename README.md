# web-app-projects
AI is becoming more capable every day, and people are now using tools like Claude for solving their problems like...
-cleaning up old TV software
-building security drones
-fixing slow routers
-Building firmware,hardware and AI assistant Microphones.

I used AI to reverse engineer existing apps, and I used Firebase for data storage. I gave the AI the relevant information and my vision of what to build, then analyzed what it produced and gave improvement prompts until the product matched what I wanted.
